# 💧 Franklin Falls

## 📊 Trail Stats
- **Location:** Snoqualmie Region
- **Popularity:** High
- **Difficulty:** Easy
- **Length:** 2.0 miles, roundtrip
- **Elevation Gain:** 400 feet
- **Criteria:** Beginner · Popular
- **Link:** https://www.wta.org/go-hiking/hikes/franklin-falls

---

## 📖 Overview

### What It Is
A popular, family-friendly waterfall hike near Snoqualmie Pass ending at a 70-foot cascade.

- Trail follows the South Fork Snoqualmie River through cedar and fir forest
- Mist and rushing water create a refreshing atmosphere at the base
- Great for families, beginners, and quick adventures

---

## ✨ Highlights

### Key Features
- 💧 Stunning 70-foot waterfall over rugged cliffs
- 🌲 Scenic forest trail with moss and river views
- 🥾 Short, beginner-friendly hike for all ages
- 📸 Great photography all year
- ❄️ Frozen waterfall views in winter

---

## 🗓 Best Time to Visit

### Great Year-Round
- **Spring:** Powerful water flow from snowmelt
- **Summer:** Warm and easy — ideal for families
- **Fall:** Golden foliage backdrop
- **Winter:** Frozen falls — traction devices may be needed

---

## 💡 Visitor Tips

### Be Prepared
- Wear sturdy footwear — trail can be muddy or slippery
- Go early or on weekdays to avoid crowds
- View the falls from safe, designated spots
- Pack out trash and follow Leave No Trace
