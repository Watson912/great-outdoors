# ⛰ Mount Si Trail

## 📊 Trail Stats
- **Location:** Snoqualmie Region
- **Popularity:** High
- **Difficulty:** Hard
- **Length:** 8.0 miles, roundtrip
- **Elevation Gain:** 3,150 feet
- **Criteria:** Advanced · Popular
- **Link:** https://www.wta.org/go-hiking/hikes/mount-si

---

## 📖 Overview

### What It Is
An iconic, challenging climb near North Bend, popular as a training hike with rewarding summit views.

- Switchbacks through evergreen forest up to rocky terrain
- Views of the Snoqualmie Valley and Cascade peaks
- Optional scramble to the Haystack near the summit

---

## ✨ Highlights

### Key Features
- 🏔 Panoramic views of the Snoqualmie Valley and Cascades
- 🌲 Forest trail lined with Douglas firs and cedars
- 🪨 Optional Haystack scramble for adventurous hikers
- 🌿 Seasonal wildflowers and abundant wildlife
- 📸 One of the most popular hikes near Seattle

---

## 🗓 Best Time to Visit

### Accessible Year-Round
- Spring/summer: lush and warm
- Fall: vibrant foliage and crisp air
- Winter: snowy scenery — needs proper gear and experience

---

## 💡 Visitor Tips

### Be Prepared
- Start early for parking, especially on weekends
- Sturdy shoes and plenty of water for the sustained climb
- Dress in layers — temps vary between trailhead and summit
- Use caution around exposed rock sections
- Follow Leave No Trace on this heavily visited trail
