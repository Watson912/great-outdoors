# 🦫 Marmot Pass Trail

## 📊 Trail Stats
- **Location:** Olympic Peninsula
- **Popularity:** Low
- **Difficulty:** Hard
- **Length:** 11.5 miles, roundtrip
- **Elevation Gain:** 3,489 feet
- **Criteria:** Advanced · Less popular
- **Link:** https://www.wta.org/go-hiking/hikes/marmot-pass-upper-big-quilcene

---

## 📖 Overview

### What It Is
A classic Olympic Peninsula hike in the Buckhorn Wilderness, climbing from forest to a high alpine saddle.

- Old-growth forest, open meadows, and rocky ridgelines
- Views of Buckhorn Mountain, Copper Peak, and the Strait of Juan de Fuca on clear days
- Named for the playful marmots often seen near the pass

---

## ✨ Highlights

### Key Features
- 🏔 Expansive alpine views of the Olympic Mountains
- 🌲 Transition from old-growth forest to subalpine meadows
- 🌸 Summer wildflowers near the pass
- 🦫 Frequent marmot sightings in rocky zones
- 📸 Excellent ridge photography spots

---

## 🗓 Best Time to Visit

### July – Early October
- Summer: most reliable conditions and full wildflower bloom
- Early fall: cooler air, fewer hikers

---

## 💡 Visitor Tips

### Be Prepared
- Long, moderately strenuous hike with steady elevation gain
- Bring layers — warm forest to windy alpine ridges
- Carry plenty of water and navigation tools
- Don't feed or approach marmots and goats
- Snow can linger at higher elevations into summer
