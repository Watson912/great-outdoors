# 🏔 Hurricane Hill Trail

## 📊 Trail Stats
- **Location:** Olympic National Park
- **Popularity:** High
- **Difficulty:** Easy
- **Length:** 3.2 miles, roundtrip
- **Elevation Gain:** 650 feet
- **Criteria:** Beginner · Popular
- **Link:** https://www.wta.org/go-hiking/hikes/hurricane-hill

---

## 📖 Overview

### What It Is
An iconic paved trail with big views for little effort, climbing gently through alpine meadows to the summit of Hurricane Hill.

- Sweeping views of the Olympic Mountains, Strait of Juan de Fuca, and Vancouver Island
- Summer wildflowers and wildlife like Olympic marmots and black-tailed deer
- Great spot for sunrise, sunset, and stargazing

---

## ✨ Highlights

### Key Features
- 🏔 Spectacular 360-degree mountain and coastal views
- 🌸 Seasonal wildflowers in alpine meadows
- 🦌 Frequent wildlife sightings
- 🚶 Smooth, paved trail for a wide range of hikers
- 📸 One of the park's most photographed viewpoints

---

## 🗓 Best Time to Visit

### Late Spring – Early Fall
- July–September has the clearest weather and best wildflowers
- Early mornings are cooler, quieter, and have softer light

---

## 💡 Visitor Tips

### Be Prepared
- Dress in layers — temperatures and winds change fast up high
- Bring water, sun protection, and a camera
- Stay on designated trails to protect alpine plants
- Check road and trail conditions before visiting
