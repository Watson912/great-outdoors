# 🌲 Hoh Rain Forest

## 📊 Trail Stats
- **Location:** Olympic National Park
- **Popularity:** High
- **Difficulty:** Easy
- **Length:** 0.8 miles loop
- **Elevation Gain:** 50 feet
- **Criteria:** Beginner · Popular
- **Link:** https://www.nps.gov/olym/planyourvisit/visiting-the-hoh.htm

---

## 📖 Overview

### What It Is
One of the largest temperate rain forests in the United States, getting up to 14 feet of rain a year.

- Towering Sitka spruce, western hemlock, Douglas fir, and bigleaf maple covered in green moss
- Fern-covered ground, clear streams, and dense canopies with soft light
- Home to Roosevelt elk, black bears, banana slugs, and many birds, plants, and fungi

---

## ✨ Highlights

### Key Features
- 🌲 Ancient trees over 300 feet tall
- 🍃 Moss-covered branches with a storybook feel
- 🥾 Easy walking trails like the Hall of Mosses and Spruce Nature Trail
- 🦌 Frequent Roosevelt elk sightings
- 🌧 Lush greenery all year long

---

## 🗓 Best Time to Visit

### Beautiful in Every Season
- **Spring:** Wildflowers bloom, rivers run high
- **Summer:** Mild and driest — best for hiking
- **Fall:** Colorful leaves, fewer visitors
- **Winter:** Mist and moss give a magical feel

---

## 💡 Visitor Tips

### Be Prepared
- Wear waterproof shoes and bring a rain jacket, even in summer
- Arrive early — parking fills fast in peak season
- Stay on designated trails to protect the ecosystem
- Keep distance from wildlife and follow Leave No Trace
