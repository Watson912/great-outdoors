# 🪨 Gothic Basin Trail

## 📊 Trail Stats
- **Location:** North Cascades
- **Popularity:** Low
- **Difficulty:** Hard
- **Length:** 9.2 miles, roundtrip
- **Elevation Gain:** 2,840 feet
- **Criteria:** Advanced · Less popular
- **Link:** https://www.wta.org/go-hiking/hikes/gothic-basin

---

## 📖 Overview

### What It Is
A rugged, dramatic alpine hike near Granite Falls, climbing steeply into a glacier-carved basin.

- Jagged peaks and granite walls around small reflective tarns
- Beneath the towering Sloan Peak
- Final section often requires scrambling over boulders and loose rock

---

## ✨ Highlights

### Key Features
- 🏔 Dramatic alpine basin with jagged peaks and cliffs
- 💧 Beautiful tarns reflecting Sloan Peak
- 🌲 Steep forest climb into high alpine terrain
- 🌸 Seasonal wildflowers among rocks and snowfields
- 📸 One of the most striking alpine spots near Seattle

---

## 🗓 Best Time to Visit

### Late July – September
- Snow mostly melted from the basin
- Early summer may still have snow crossings; fall is cooler and quieter

---

## 💡 Visitor Tips

### Be Prepared
- Strenuous hike with big elevation gain and rocky scrambling
- Sturdy boots with good traction for unstable terrain
- Extra layers — the basin can be cold and windy even in summer
- Carry navigation tools and check conditions first
- Follow Leave No Trace — fragile alpine environment
