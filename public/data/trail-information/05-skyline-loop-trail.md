# 🌋 Skyline Loop Trail

## 📊 Trail Stats
- **Location:** Mount Rainier National Park
- **Popularity:** High
- **Difficulty:** Moderate
- **Length:** 5.5 miles loop
- **Elevation Gain:** 1,450 feet
- **Criteria:** Beginner+Advanced · Popular
- **Link:** https://www.wta.org/go-hiking/hikes/skyline-trail-loop

---

## 📖 Overview

### What It Is
A premier loop hike starting at the Paradise area, climbing through subalpine landscapes with views of Mount Rainier.

- Wildflower meadows, waterfalls, and glacier views
- Marmots, mountain goats, and many birds along the way
- One of the most photographed trails in the Pacific Northwest

---

## ✨ Highlights

### Key Features
- 🏔 Close-up views of Mount Rainier and its glaciers
- 🌸 Alpine meadows full of seasonal wildflowers
- 💧 Scenic stops like Myrtle Falls and mountain streams
- 🦌 Marmots, mountain goats, and native wildlife
- 📸 Panoramic viewpoints over valleys and peaks

---

## 🗓 Best Time to Visit

### Mid-July – September
- Snow-free with peak wildflowers and best weather
- Early mornings: cooler, fewer crowds, best photos

---

## 💡 Visitor Tips

### Be Prepared
- Sturdy shoes and layers — mountain weather changes fast
- Bring water, snacks, and sun protection (lots of exposure)
- Stay on designated paths to protect the alpine ecosystem
- Check trail and weather conditions — snow can linger into summer
