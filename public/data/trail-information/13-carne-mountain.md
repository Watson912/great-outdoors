# 🐐 Carne Mountain

## 📊 Trail Stats
- **Location:** Central Cascades
- **Popularity:** Low
- **Difficulty:** Hard
- **Length:** 7.3 miles, roundtrip
- **Elevation Gain:** 3,600 feet
- **Criteria:** Advanced · Less popular
- **Link:** https://www.wta.org/go-hiking/hikes/carne-mountain

---

## 📖 Overview

### What It Is
A striking alpine hike in the eastern North Cascades near the Entiat River Valley with long ridgeline views.

- Forested slopes giving way to subalpine wildflowers and rocky outcrops
- Views of Glacier Peak and the rugged Entiat Mountains
- Broad, exposed ridge feels remote — no technical climbing required

---

## ✨ Highlights

### Key Features
- 🏔 Wide-open ridge views of Glacier Peak and the Cascades
- 🌸 Vibrant subalpine wildflowers mid–late summer
- 🌲 Scenic transition from forest to open alpine terrain
- 📸 Panoramic viewpoints with minimal crowds
- 🐾 Mountain goats and other alpine wildlife

---

## 🗓 Best Time to Visit

### July – September
- Snow melted from the ridge, wildflowers in bloom
- Early season: lingering snow patches; fall: crisp air, quieter trails

---

## 💡 Visitor Tips

### Be Prepared
- Long, strenuous hike — start early and pace yourself
- Bring layers for the windy, exposed ridge
- Plenty of water and sun protection (limited shade up high)
- Check trail conditions after snowmelt or storms
- Stay on established paths to protect alpine plants
