# 💎 Colchuck Lake Trail

## 📊 Trail Stats
- **Location:** Central Cascades
- **Popularity:** High
- **Difficulty:** Moderate/Hard
- **Length:** 8.0 miles, roundtrip
- **Elevation Gain:** 2,280 feet
- **Criteria:** Advanced · Popular
- **Link:** https://www.wta.org/trail-news/go-hiking/hikes/colchuck-lake

---

## 📖 Overview

### What It Is
An alpine adventure leading to one of Washington's most stunning glacial lakes, at the edge of the Enchantments.

- Pine forests and granite landscapes leading to turquoise water
- Framed by the towering peaks of the Stuart Range
- Popular with day hikers and photographers

---

## ✨ Highlights

### Key Features
- 💎 Vibrant turquoise glacial lake with granite peaks
- 🏔 Views of Dragontail Peak and the Stuart Range
- 🌲 Forest trails with streams and wildflowers
- 📸 Excellent reflection photography
- 🥾 Gateway to the famous Enchantments wilderness

---

## 🗓 Best Time to Visit

### Late June – October
- Summer: warm hiking and wildflowers
- Early fall: crisp air, golden larches, colorful scenery

---

## 💡 Visitor Tips

### Be Prepared
- Start early — parking fills quickly in peak season
- Sturdy shoes for rocky, uneven terrain
- Bring water, snacks, and layers
- Follow Leave No Trace in the fragile alpine area
- Check trail conditions and required permits before going
