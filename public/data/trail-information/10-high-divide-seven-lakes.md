# 🏕 High Divide – Seven Lakes Basin Loop

## 📊 Trail Stats
- **Location:** Olympic National Park
- **Popularity:** High
- **Difficulty:** Hard
- **Length:** 19.0 miles, roundtrip
- **Elevation Gain:** 4,000 feet
- **Criteria:** Advanced · Advanced
- **Link:** https://www.wta.org/news/go-hiking/hikes/seven-lakes-basin

---

## 📖 Overview

### What It Is
One of the most spectacular backcountry routes in Olympic National Park, mixing mountain ridgelines with alpine lakes.

- Climbs from rainforest to high alpine terrain
- Views of the Olympic Mountains, including Mount Olympus on clear days
- Popular multi-day backpacking trip; sometimes done as a long day hike

---

## ✨ Highlights

### Key Features
- 🏔 Ridge views of the Olympics and Mount Olympus
- 💧 Seven alpine lakes in glacial basins
- 🌲 Transition from rainforest to alpine ecosystems
- 🦌 Black bears, marmots, and deer in remote habitat
- 📸 Exceptional backpacking scenery

---

## 🗓 Best Time to Visit

### Mid-July – September
- Summer: best for hiking and camping, wildflowers, long daylight
- Early fall: cooler temps and fewer crowds
- Snow can linger into early summer up high

---

## 💡 Visitor Tips

### Be Prepared
- Wilderness permit required for overnight backpacking
- Expect big elevation gain and changing mountain weather
- Carry bear-proof food storage; follow Leave No Trace strictly
- Bring layers for rainforest humidity and alpine wind
- Reserve campsites in advance during peak season
