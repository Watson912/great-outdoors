# 🛖 Mount Fremont Lookout Trail

## 📊 Trail Stats
- **Location:** Mount Rainier National Park
- **Popularity:** High
- **Difficulty:** Moderate
- **Length:** 5.6 miles, roundtrip
- **Elevation Gain:** 1,200 feet
- **Criteria:** Beginner+Advanced · Popular
- **Link:** https://www.wta.org/go-hiking/hikes/mount-fremont-lookout

---

## 📖 Overview

### What It Is
A classic alpine hike from the Sunrise area to a historic fire lookout high above the landscape.

- Ridgelines and open meadows with constant Mount Rainier views
- Summer wildflowers, mountain goats, marmots, and birds of prey
- Restored lookout with 360-degree views of the Cascades

---

## ✨ Highlights

### Key Features
- 🏔 Uninterrupted views of Mount Rainier and glaciers
- 🛖 Historic fire lookout with panoramic scenery
- 🌸 Alpine meadows and seasonal wildflowers
- 🦌 Frequent marmot and mountain goat sightings
- 📸 Exceptional sunrise and sunset photography

---

## 🗓 Best Time to Visit

### July – Early October
- Snow melted and alpine flowers in bloom
- Late afternoon/evening hikes are popular for golden light and sunsets

---

## 💡 Visitor Tips

### Be Prepared
- Bring warm layers — cool temps and strong winds even in summer
- Carry water, snacks, and sun protection (exposed trail)
- Stay on designated paths to protect alpine plants
- Check conditions — mountain weather changes fast
