# 🍂 Maple Pass Loop

## 📊 Trail Stats
- **Location:** North Cascades
- **Popularity:** High
- **Difficulty:** Moderate/Hard
- **Length:** 7.2 miles, roundtrip
- **Elevation Gain:** 2,020 feet
- **Criteria:** Advanced · Popular
- **Link:** https://www.wta.org/go-hiking/hikes/maple-pass

---

## 📖 Overview

### What It Is
One of Washington's most celebrated alpine hikes, climbing through forest to open ridgelines with views in every direction.

- Crystal-clear lakes, rugged peaks, and colorful meadows
- Famous for golden larches and fall colors
- Summer wildflowers, marmots, and mountain goats

---

## ✨ Highlights

### Key Features
- 🏔 Panoramic views of the North Cascades
- 🍂 Stunning golden larches and fall foliage
- 💧 Overlooks of Lake Ann and alpine tarns
- 🌸 Colorful wildflower meadows in summer
- 📸 Premier landscape photography destination

---

## 🗓 Best Time to Visit

### Late June – October
- Summer: lush meadows and clear views
- Late September – early October: famous golden larch season

---

## 💡 Visitor Tips

### Be Prepared
- Hike clockwise for a more gradual climb and steady views
- Bring layers — weather changes fast even in summer
- Carry water, snacks, and sun protection for exposed sections
- Stay on marked trails and follow Leave No Trace
