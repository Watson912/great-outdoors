# 🌄 Ptarmigan Ridge Trail

## 📊 Trail Stats
- **Location:** North Cascades
- **Popularity:** Low
- **Difficulty:** Hard
- **Length:** 9.0 miles, roundtrip
- **Elevation Gain:** 1,800 feet
- **Criteria:** Advanced · Less popular
- **Link:** https://www.wta.org/go-hiking/hikes/ptarmigan-ridge

---

## 📖 Overview

### What It Is
One of Washington's most spectacular high-alpine hikes, in the Mount Baker Wilderness.

- Exposed ridgelines and alpine meadows starting near Artist Point
- Mount Baker and the Coleman Glacier dominate the views
- On clear days, see deep into the North Cascades and across to the Olympics

---

## ✨ Highlights

### Key Features
- 🏔 Constant views of Mount Baker and the Coleman Glacier
- 🌄 Expansive alpine ridgelines with dramatic drop-offs
- 🌸 Seasonal wildflowers across high meadows
- ❄️ Lingering snowfields well into summer
- 📸 One of the most photogenic ridge hikes in Washington

---

## 🗓 Best Time to Visit

### July – September
- Early season may still have significant snow
- Late summer: best balance of clear trails and vibrant scenery

---

## 💡 Visitor Tips

### Be Prepared
- Be ready for snow travel early season (traction devices, trekking poles)
- Warm layers and wind protection — constant exposure on the ridge
- Carry navigation tools — snow can hide parts of the trail
- Start early to avoid afternoon weather changes
- Follow Leave No Trace in fragile alpine areas
