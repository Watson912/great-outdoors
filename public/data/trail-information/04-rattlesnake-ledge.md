# 🏞 Rattlesnake Ledge

## 📊 Trail Stats
- **Location:** Snoqualmie Region
- **Popularity:** High
- **Difficulty:** Moderate
- **Length:** 4.0 miles, roundtrip
- **Elevation Gain:** 1,160 feet
- **Criteria:** Beginner+Advanced · Popular
- **Link:** https://www.wta.org/go-hiking/hikes/rattlesnake-ledge

---

## 📖 Overview

### What It Is
One of Washington's most iconic day hikes, just a short drive from Seattle, climbing through forest to a dramatic rocky ledge.

- Views of Rattlesnake Lake and the Cascade foothills
- Well-maintained path through Douglas firs and hemlocks
- Suitable for hikers of all experience levels

---

## ✨ Highlights

### Key Features
- 🏞 Panoramic overlook of Rattlesnake Lake
- 🌲 Towering evergreens and seasonal wildflowers
- 🥾 Well-maintained path for beginners and experienced hikers
- 📸 One of the most photographed viewpoints near Seattle
- 🌅 Beautiful sunrise and sunset views

---

## 🗓 Best Time to Visit

### Accessible Year-Round
- Spring/summer: green and warm
- Fall: colorful foliage
- Winter: peaceful but possibly icy

---

## 💡 Visitor Tips

### Be Prepared
- Arrive early or go on weekdays to avoid crowds
- Wear sturdy shoes and bring plenty of water
- Stay behind safety barriers — avoid unstable cliff edges
- Keep pets leashed and follow Leave No Trace
- Check weather and trail conditions, especially in winter
